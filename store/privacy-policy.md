# Privacy Policy — Nabi ur Rahmah ﷺ

*Last updated: 1 October 2026*

Nabi ur Rahmah ("the app") is published free of charge by **Dar-ul-Huda Charitable Trust, Udupi, India** ("we").

## The short version
The app does not ask for, collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-up, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

## What stays on your phone
- Books you choose to download, the page you last read, and your settings (such as whether the salawat plays on opening) are saved **only on your phone**. Uninstalling the app removes them.
- Images you choose to save are placed in your phone's gallery or Downloads folder.

## What the app downloads
To show its content the app connects to:
- **GitHub** (raw.githubusercontent.com, api.github.com), where Dar-ul-Huda publishes the flyers, books and the list of videos;
- **IslamHouse.com**, for Seerah books in many languages;
- **darulhudaudupi.org**, Dar-ul-Huda's website;
- **YouTube**, to play videos in the official YouTube player.

Like any website, these services receive standard technical information needed to deliver the content, such as your IP address and device type. We do not receive or keep it. Videos are played by YouTube under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Ratings
The app may occasionally show Google Play's own rating window. Ratings are handled entirely by Google Play.

## Sharing
When you share a flyer, video or book, the app hands it to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp). What happens next is governed by that app.

## Children
The app contains religious educational material suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
