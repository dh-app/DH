# Play Console answers

Answers for the forms under **Policy → App content**, based on what the app actually does.

## Privacy policy
`https://github.com/dh-app/DH/blob/main/store/privacy-policy.md`

## Ads
**No**, the app does not contain ads.

## App access
**All functionality is available without special access** (no login).

## Content rating (IARC questionnaire)
- Category: **Reference, News, or Educational**
- Violence, sexuality, language, controlled substances, gambling: **No** to every question
- Users can interact or exchange content with other users: **No** (sharing to WhatsApp etc. happens in other apps)
- Shares the user's location: **No**
- Allows purchases of digital goods: **No**

Expected rating: **Everyone / PEGI 3**.

## Target audience and content
- Target age groups: **13–15, 16–17, 18 and over**
- Choosing ages under 13 brings Google's Families policy, which this first release doesn't need.
- Appeals to children: **No**.

## Data safety
- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No**
- Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? Not applicable (no data collected); all connections use HTTPS.
- Do you provide a way for users to request that their data be deleted? Not applicable.

Result shown on the store: **"No data collected · No data shared with third parties"**.

## Government app / Financial features / Health / News
**No** to each.

## Photo and video permissions
The app doesn't request them. It saves images through Android's MediaStore, which needs no permission. Older Android 9 and below use the storage permission only to save to Downloads.
