## Full description (max 4000)
```
Nabi ur Rahmah ﷺ — "the Prophet of Mercy" — brings the life, character and teachings of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ to you in your own language. Read, watch, save and share, completely free, with no ads and no sign-up.

📜 TEACHINGS IN FLYERS — 18 LANGUAGES
Beautifully designed flyers in Urdu, Hindi, English, Arabic, Kannada, Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Gujarati, Marathi, Punjabi, Odia, Nepali, Turkish, French, German, Italian and more.
• Crisp, full-quality images you can zoom into
• Save to your gallery in one tap
• Share straight to WhatsApp, or anywhere else

🎬 TEACHINGS IN VIDEOS
Short, powerful Hadith series in English and Urdu.
• Plays smoothly inside the app, upright for Shorts
• Keeps playing in a floating window when you switch apps
• Share any video with family and friends

📖 BIOGRAPHY OF PROPHET MUHAMMAD ﷺ
Authentic Seerah books in many languages, from IslamHouse.com and Dar-ul-Huda.
• A comfortable built-in reader: night mode, go to page, pinch to zoom
• Picks up exactly where you stopped
• Download for offline reading, or share a book

📚 MORE BOOKS, AND WHAT OTHERS SAY ABOUT HIM ﷺ
New collections are added regularly. They appear in the app by themselves, with no update needed.

✨ MADE WITH CARE
• Opens with the Durood and a gentle salawat (can be switched off)
• Fast, light and works offline for everything you have already opened
• Light and dark themes, right-to-left scripts and screen-reader support
• No ads, no account, no tracking

From Dar-ul-Huda Charitable Trust, Udupi. May Allah accept this effort and make it a means of guidance.

Contact us from inside the app, or visit darulhudaudupi.org
```
